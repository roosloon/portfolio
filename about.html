Portfolio About

I am both a conceptual thinker and a doer;

I graduated from Eindhoven University of Technology with master’s degree in Industrial Design . During my studies, I built a foundation in product design and learned to combine creative exploration with a research-driven approach.

Today, I bring over four years of design experience in multidisciplinary and international environments, contributing to the development of early-stage ideas into commercially relevant products and services.

My approach is creative, human-centred, and evidence-based. I bring a practical toolkit across design thinking, human-centred research, and growth methods. I enjoy translating data into actionable insights that help organizations grow alongside their users and (future) markets.
