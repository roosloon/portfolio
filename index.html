Home Office Sound Zone - The Restorative Potential of Spatial Soundscapes

The Home Office Sound Zone is a headphone-based spatial soundscape system designed to help young professional knowledge workers mentally separate work from home. My master thesis explored how spatial audio and nature-based soundscapes could support psychological detachment, recovery, and focus within the home office environment.

Womb Phone for the Artificial Womb (exhibited at Dutch Design Week, 2020)

The Womb Phone supports bonding between fathers and preterm babies in an artificial womb, a radically new alternative to the neonatal intensive care unit. Through a specially designed speaker, parents can call their baby and share their voices remotely, helping both feel more connected.
